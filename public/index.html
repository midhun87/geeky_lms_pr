The Brand Story

Every Great Journey Begins With a Dream.

Geeky Researcher began with two students, Nirmal Raj and N.H. Shankar Reddy, who shared a vision of learning, excellence, and making quality knowledge accessible to others.

Over time, this vision developed into a focused educational initiative in Plant Science and Plant Pathology. Through educational videos, concept explanations, research-oriented learning, and the trust of subscribers, the initiative grew into a dedicated learning ecosystem under the Geeky Researcher brand.

Our Mission

To create a focused, reliable, and academically strong learning ecosystem for Plant Pathology. We believe Plant Pathology should be understood, not memorized.
